About

This site describes the small publishing app "ngernnaihua API" that we use to upload videos, Shorts, Reels and posts to the ngernnaihua YouTube channel and Facebook Page, which we own. The app connects to the YouTube Data API and the Facebook Graph API only on behalf of our own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not collect data from viewers.
