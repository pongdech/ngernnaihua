About

This site describes the small publishing apps (for example "SiamOnMap API" and "EnglishRodTai API") that we use to upload videos, Shorts, Reels and posts to channels we own or manage. The apps connect to the YouTube Data API and the Facebook Graph API only on behalf of our own accounts. They are not offered to the public and do not collect data from viewers.
