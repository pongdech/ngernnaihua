1. Who we are

This policy covers "ngernnaihua API", the publishing app operated by the owner of the ngernnaihua channel ("we") for our own YouTube channel and Facebook Page.

2. What the app does

The app uploads videos and sets titles, descriptions, tags, playlists and thumbnails on the ngernnaihua channel and Page. It is used only by us and is not offered to other users.

3. Data we access

  • YouTube (Google): through the YouTube API Services we access our own channel's basic information and the videos we upload, using the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl.
  • Facebook (Meta): through the Graph API we access our own Page, publish posts and videos to it, and read engagement on our own posts.
  • Access tokens: OAuth tokens that let the app act on our own accounts.

We do not access, collect or store personal data of viewers, followers or any other users.

4. How we use and store data

Data is used only to publish and manage our own content. Access tokens are stored privately on our own computers and are never published. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

5. YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

You can revoke the app's access to a Google account at any time on the Google security settings page.

6. Facebook and data deletion

Access to Facebook can be removed at any time in Facebook Settings → Business integrations, by removing the app. To ask us to delete any data related to this app, email us at the address below. We will delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.
